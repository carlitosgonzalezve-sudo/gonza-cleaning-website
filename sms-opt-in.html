SMS Opt-In and Consent

GONZA Cleaning Services LLC sends text messages only to customers who agree to receive them. This page shows exactly how customers opt in on our estimate form.

Effective November 18, 2026

How customers opt in

Customers request a free cleaning estimate with the form at gonzacleaning.com. At the end of the form (step 3) there are two separate checkboxes. Both are optional and unchecked by default. Text messaging consent is not a condition of purchase.

Program details

Sender: GONZA Cleaning Services LLC, Raleigh, North Carolina.

Message types: estimate follow-ups, appointment confirmations and reminders, and service updates (service messages); and, only if you separately opt in, promotional offers and discounts.

Frequency: message frequency varies. Rates: message and data rates may apply.

Opt out: reply STOP at any time to stop receiving text messages. Reply HELP for help or contact us at contact@gonzacleaning.com or (984) 384-5742.

Privacy: no mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy and Terms.
