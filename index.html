What is the difference between deep and recurring cleaning?

Deep Cleaning is the detailed reset for buildup and overlooked areas. Recurring Cleaning maintains that result weekly, biweekly, or monthly with consistent care for everyday living spaces.

Do you clean offices and commercial spaces?

Yes. We create cleaning plans for offices and commercial spaces based on the property size, layout, traffic, priorities, and preferred service frequency.

Which service is best for a move?

Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning is designed for an empty property, when cabinets, closets, floors, and other surfaces are fully accessible. Share your timing and property details so we can tailor the estimate.

Do you bring cleaning products and equipment?

Yes. GONZA brings the cleaning products and professional equipment needed for the scheduled service. Tell us in advance about any product preferences or sensitive surfaces.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Send the property size, ZIP code, service needed, and any special priorities. We’ll review the details and follow up with a personalized, no-pressure estimate.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not necessarily. Access arrangements can be discussed and confirmed with you before the visit so the plan is clear and comfortable for everyone.

How do I know whether my address is covered?

Review the service cities listed above, then include your ZIP code in the form. We’ll confirm availability for your exact address before scheduling.
